EXECUTIVE PROFILE & PHILOSOPHY

Legal Acumen Meets Commercial Pragmatism

Arjun Kshetri is a qualified lawyer, legal strategist, and business advisor with over a decade of cross-border experience spanning courtroom litigation, corporate governance, and management consulting. Enrolled with the Bar Council of West Bengal and educated in the United Kingdom, he works alongside startups, SMEs, family enterprises, and multinational organizations to navigate high-stakes legal disputes and complex commercial decisions.

Having transitioned from active litigation to corporate boardrooms, he combines sharp legal acumen with commercial pragmatism—helping founders and executives align strategy, regulation, and organizational structure from idea stage to exit.

In Indian jurisdictions, Arjun represents clients in critical constitutional litigation, civil suits, and criminal bail proceedings across trial courts, High Courts (Calcutta, Delhi, Bombay), and the Supreme Court of India. In the UK and UAE corridors, he advises entrepreneurial businesses on structural formation, seed and venture financing rounds, shareholder dynamics, and cross-border M&A.
